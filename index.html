01 / What it is

A payment rail for things that run themselves.

A delivery drone pays a rooftop charger. A car pays a toll gate. An AI agent pays another agent for data. Kelvara settles each of these in USDT, in under a second, and nobody has to approve anything.

412 ms median settlement

Payments clear between two devices before the charger has finished saying hello. Finality comes from relays that batch to TON every block.

USDT in, USDT out

Devices hold and spend a stable currency. No volatile gas token on the machine.

Keys live on the device

Every machine signs with its own hardware key. The owner sets a spending limit and the chain enforces it.

$0.0004

Fees too small to meter

The average payment costs four hundredths of a cent, so a sensor can sell one reading at a time and still come out ahead.

02 / How it works

Open, pay, settle.

channel open · limit 25.00 USDT0.42 USDT streamedbatch 48,211 · 1,204 payments · final in 412 ms

  1. 1

    Open a channel

    The drone and the charger have never met. They open a payment channel with a spending limit the drone's owner set in advance. One on-chain message, a few hundred milliseconds.

  2. 2

    Pay as you go

    While it charges, the drone streams USDT every second for the energy it has actually drawn. These payments are signed by the two devices and cost nothing to send.

  3. 3

    Settle in batches

    Relays roll thousands of channel updates into one batch and write it to TON. The charger's balance is final, and anyone can check the proof.

03 / The token

KVRA keeps the relays honest.

  • A

    Stake to run a relay

    Relays post KVRA as a bond. A relay that signs a bad batch loses it.

  • B

    Earn the settlement fee

    Every batch pays its relay in USDT, shared with the people who staked behind it.

  • C

    Vote on the limits

    Holders set batch size, fee floors and which device standards the network accepts.
